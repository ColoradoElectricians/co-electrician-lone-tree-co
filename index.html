Before work begins

Plan around the property, not assumptions

Two properties can have very different electrical layouts, even when the requested work sounds similar. The age and condition of the installation, panel capacity, equipment being added and project scope all influence the right approach.

For work within Lone Tree, confirm the property's jurisdiction and review the official City of Lone Tree website for current building and permit information. Requirements can vary by project, so verify them before work starts.

Safety reminder: Burning smells, sparks, hot outlets or unusual buzzing from a panel should not be ignored. Keep clear of damaged electrical equipment and seek qualified help. If there is smoke or fire, leave the area and call emergency services.

Helpful answers

Frequently asked questions about electrical work in Lone Tree

What are signs that an electrical issue needs attention?

Repeated breaker trips, flickering lights, buzzing sounds, warm outlets, burning odours or unexplained power loss should be assessed by a qualified electrician. If there is smoke or fire, move away and call emergency services.

Can I add an EV charger to my Lone Tree home?

An electrician can assess the electrical panel, available capacity, charger location and circuit requirements before installing a home charging station. See our EV charger installation information.

When might an electrical panel need an upgrade?

A panel assessment may be appropriate when adding major appliances, planning a remodel, installing an EV charger or dealing with recurring breaker trips. An electrician can evaluate the load and recommend suitable next steps.

Do electrical projects in Lone Tree need permits?

Permit requirements depend on the project and property's jurisdiction. Check the City of Lone Tree's official website for current guidance before work begins.

Should I keep resetting a breaker that trips?

No. A breaker that repeatedly trips may be responding to an overload or fault. Avoid repeatedly resetting it and arrange for the cause to be assessed.

What details should I provide when contacting an electrician?

Explain the symptom, when it occurs, which rooms or devices are affected and whether anything has changed recently. Never remove electrical covers or expose wiring to gather information.
